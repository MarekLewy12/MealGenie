MealGenie — new logo concepts

Three directions that fit Direction A's cozy Polish kitchen aesthetic. Each ditches the gradient genie + star (which reads premium-tech, not warm) for something hand-drawn and food-first. All work on cream and dark backgrounds.

Concept 01 · Pierogi mark
MealGenie
Fraunces 500 · italic accent on Genie

A single golden-pinched pierogi inside a terracotta circle, with two basil flecks. Reads as "homemade Polish food" instantly. Works at favicon size.

MealGenie
Cookbook plate motif

A wooden spoon centered like a serif glyph in a rounded plate. Feels like a stamp on a recipe binder. Most flexible — works at any size.

MealGenie

Dark mode · all three
MealGenie

Terracotta brightens to #e88a4a on dark; basil shifts to #7ab068. Saffron stays. The wordmark italic on "Genie" is the signature regardless of mark.

Recommendation: Concept 02 (Spoon monogram) — most legible at small sizes, most flexible across product surfaces, and the "stamp" feel reinforces the cookbook metaphor. Concept 01 is more distinctive but harder to use small.
